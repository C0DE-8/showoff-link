1. Information We Collect

At Showoff Links, we collect information to provide better services to our users. We gather details in the following ways:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, and password when you register.
  • Profile & Content Data: Custom URLs, profile photos, bio descriptions, and the links you choose to showcase.
  • Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, referrer URLs, and interaction data (e.g., link click rates).

2. How We Use Information

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our link-sharing platform.
  • To generate public profile pages based on your submitted content.
  • To provide analytics on link performance and traffic.
  • To communicate account updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, store session preferences, and understand user behavior. You can configure your browser to decline cookies, though certain interactive features of Showoff Links may not function properly as a result.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data under the following circumstances:

  • Public Profiles: Any links or profile content you explicitly publish are publicly accessible on the internet.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors (such as cloud hosting or database services) bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government regulation.

5. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no internet transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access, update, or delete your profile information via your account dashboard.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Delete your entire account and associated link profiles at any time.
